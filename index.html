Syllable weight

Short and long

Say the words aloud. A short open syllable usually counts as short. A long vowel or a short vowel closed by a consonant usually makes a syllable long. Some weights can vary under arūz conventions.

1 Short

نَ · न · na

2 Long

دل · दिल · dil

Rukn
One metrical foot, made of a fixed sequence of weights. Its plural is arkaan.
Behr
The meter. Its feet give the pattern for each misra (line) of the sher (couplet).
Taqtī‘
Scansion. Mark the weights as spoken, then group them into feet.

Common behers

11212Mutafā‘ilun11212Mutafā‘ilun11212Mutafā‘ilun11212Mutafā‘ilun

Each foot opens with two short syllables. In Urdu examples, two initial shorts can also be replaced by one long.

  • کبھی اے حقیقتِ منتظر نظر آ لباسِ مجاز میںکہ ہزاروں سجدے تڑپ رہے ہیں مری جبینِ نیاز میں
    कभी ऐ हक़ीक़त-ए-मुंतज़र नज़र आ लिबास-ए-मजाज़ मेंकि हज़ारों सज्दे तड़प रहे हैं मिरी जबीन-ए-नियाज़ में
    Kabhī ai haqīqat-e-muntazar nazar ā libās-e-majāz meñKi hazāroñ sajde taṛap rahe haiñ mirī jabīn-e-niyāz meñ
    Allama IqbalSource for Kāmil example 1
  • وہ جو ہم میں تم میں قرار تھا تمہیں یاد ہو کہ نہ یاد ہووہی یعنی وعدہ نباہ کا تمہیں یاد ہو کہ نہ یاد ہو
    वो जो हम में तुम में क़रार था तुम्हें याद हो कि न याद होवही यानी वादा निबाह का तुम्हें याद हो कि न याद हो

Taqtī‘

Scan a misra

Say Dil-e nādāñ tujhe huā kyā hai aloud. Mark each syllable 1 or 2, then see how they fit into the three feet of Khafīf.

Reveal the scansion
2122dil-e nā-dāñ1212tu-jhe hu-ā22kyā hai

In dil-e, the linking -e is short here. Both kyā and hai count as long at the end.
